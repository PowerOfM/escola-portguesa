To consolidate the foundations of the Portuguese language and expand the ability to communicate in simple everyday situations, with greater autonomy and accuracy.

### Vocabulary
- Greetings and introductions
- School routines
- Healthy eating
- Clothing and accessories
- Transportation and directions
- Leisure activities and sports
- Hobbies and free time

### Grammar
- Review and consolidation of basic verbs (ser, estar, ter, ir).
- Most commonly used regular and irregular verbs in the present tense.
- Introduction to the simple past of the most frequent verbs (perfect tense).
- Common prepositions: em, de, para, por, com.
- Adverbs of time and frequency.
- Building longer sentences using and, but, because.
- Questions using “what,” “where,” “when,” “how,” and “who.”

### Teaching Methods
- Frequent oral practice (dialogues, role-plays, interviews).
- Guided reading of short texts.
- Listening activities with clear, paced speech.
- Guided written production with progressive correction.
- Language games, vocabulary cards, and simulations.
