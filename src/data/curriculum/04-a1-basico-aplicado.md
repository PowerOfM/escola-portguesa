### 1. Listening Comprehension
- Understand simple oral messages and instructions in authentic contexts.
- Recognize key information in short dialogues or announcements.
- Identify basic personal and factual details (names, dates, times, places).

### 2. Speaking / Oral Expression
- Introduce themselves and others and greet appropriately.
- Ask and answer simple questions about daily life, routines, and preferences.
- Express needs, likes and dislikes, and basic opinions.
- Participate in short conversations and on a variety of topics.

### 3. Reading Comprehension
- Understand short, simple fictional and non-fictional texts, and dialogues.
- Identify main ideas and specific information in signs, notices, or instructions.
- Read short narrative texts (like short stories and poems) with support.

### 4. Writing / Written Expression
- Write short sentences and simple paragraphs about themselves, family, hobbies, and
daily routines.
- Write simple letters and postcards.
- Fill in forms with personal information (name, age, address, phone number).
- Use basic connectors and sentence structures to describe actions or events.

### 5. Grammar and Vocabulary Development
- Use present tense verbs and basic sentence structures with regular ar, er, and ir
ending verbs.
- Correctly apply articles, prepositions of place and time, and pronouns in context.
- Expand everyday vocabulary related to home, school, work, leisure, food, and culture.
